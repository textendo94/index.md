# Privacy Policy — PrepMoney

**Last updated: April 8, 2026**

## Data Collection
PrepMoney does not collect any personal data. All data entered in the app (expenses, budgets, goals) is stored locally on your device and synced via your personal iCloud account.

## iCloud Sync
If you have iCloud enabled, your data is synced through Apple's iCloud service. This data is only accessible to you and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

## In-App Purchases
PrepMoney uses Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases. No payment information is processed or stored by us. All transactions are handled by Apple.

## No Third Parties
PrepMoney does not use any analytics, advertising networks, or third-party SDKs.
